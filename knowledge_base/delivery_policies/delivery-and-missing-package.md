# Delivery and missing package policy

## Tracking
Customers can use the carrier status and estimated delivery shown in their order details. An estimate is not a guaranteed delivery date.

## Marked delivered but not received
Ask the customer to check the delivery location, household members, building reception, and nearby safe places. If the parcel is still missing, create a support case for investigation. Do not claim that a refund or replacement is approved before eligibility review.

## Delayed delivery
When a parcel is still in transit, share the latest verified carrier event and estimate. Escalate if tracking has not updated for more than five calendar days.
